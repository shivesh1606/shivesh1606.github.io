Echoes of History Archive

Short, sourced history videos in English and Hindi.

Echoes of History Archive publishes short educational videos about world history: ancient civilisations, inventions and turning points. Each video takes under a minute and tells one story.

How the videos are made

  • The script is written from a Wikipedia article, which is credited in every video description.
  • A second AI model checks the script against that article.
  • The images are AI-generated, and every video is labelled as altered or synthetic content on YouTube.
  • A person reviews each video before it is published.

Echoes of History Uploader

The channel uses a small private tool, the Echoes of History Uploader, to upload reviewed videos to its own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. Only the channel owner uses it; it does not offer sign-in to anyone else. The privacy policy explains what data it uses.
