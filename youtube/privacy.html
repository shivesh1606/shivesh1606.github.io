Privacy policy

Echoes of History Uploader · effective 20 November 2026

The Echoes of History Uploader ("the app") is a private tool that the owner of the Echoes of History Archive YouTube channel uses to upload that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public, and only the channel owner signs in to it.

Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

With the account holder's permission, given on Google's sign-in screen, the app uses:

  • Upload access (youtube.upload): to upload videos to the signed-in channel and to set their title, description, tags, privacy and AI-content label.
  • Read-only access (youtube.readonly): to read the signed-in channel's name and ID, which confirms the channel the videos go to.

The app does not read comments, subscribers, viewing history, messages or any other account data, and it never accesses another person's account.

What data is stored

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, so that the app can upload without a new sign-in every time. It is never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • The video ID and link of each upload are saved in the app's private database as a record of what was published. They are kept for as long as the channel exists.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, transfer or disclose any data from YouTube or Google to anyone else, and it does not use that data for advertising. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

You can remove the app's access at any time in the security settings of your Google account, at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have the stored video records deleted, email the address below. They are deleted within 30 days.

Cookies

This website and the app do not use cookies or analytics.

Changes
